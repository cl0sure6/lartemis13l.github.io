**Preprint, September 2026.**

## Overview

A structural note on Tao's averaged Navier–Stokes equation. The main result is that his blowup operator does not arise as the Euler–Arnold operator of any translation-covariant inertia operator: because his profiles all live in one thin annulus, a cascade triple is compatible with the Euler bracket only if it carries three distinct species, and twelve of the sixteen non-zero structure constants of his Table 1 do not. A companion rigidity theorem shows that on SDiff(T³) the energy-preserving triad reweightings of Euler that remain Euler–Arnold are exactly the global rescalings, while the same notion yields a two-parameter family in the two-dimensional Zeitlin truncation.
